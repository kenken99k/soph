Contact Us and Send a Pre-Order Request

The quickest way to reach us is the form below. We read every request and reply within one business day to confirm your order, the final price, and a pickup time. For same-day needs, please call the shop instead — our ovens cannot always keep up with same-day celebration cakes.

    Visit the Shop

    North Star Bakery
    418 Alder Street, Brookfield
    Phone: (555) 014-2288
    Email: hello@northstarbakery.example

    We are on the corner of Alder Street and Northstar Lane, two doors down from the community library. There is a small free lot behind the building, and the 14 bus stops directly in front of the shop. The entrance is step-free, the counter is at a lowered section for wheelchair access, and our restrooms are single-occupancy and accessible.

    Hours of Operation

    When the shop and the bakery counter are open
    Day Shop hours Fresh bakes available
    Monday – Friday 7:00 a.m. – 3:00 p.m. Bread from 7:00 a.m., pastries from 7:30 a.m.
    Saturday 7:00 a.m. – 2:00 p.m. Bread and pastries from 7:00 a.m. until sold out
    Sunday 8:00 a.m. – 12:00 p.m. Bread only, from 8:00 a.m.

    We are closed Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day. Holiday hours are posted on the door and in the weekly bake list. During severe weather we close early when the roads are unsafe, and we will post a notice at the door and on the website.

    Other Ways to Reach Us

    • Call the shop for same-day bread, rolls, and pastries. We take orders by phone up to the hour before we close.
    • Walk in any time during shop hours. We do not take walk-in celebration cake orders, but we will happily write a message on one you bring in yourself.
    • Email for catering quotes, invoices, and feedback. Expect a reply within one business day.

    Questions about our breads, pastries, and current pricing are answered on the products page, and the story of the bakery and our suppliers is on the about page.
