Our Breads, Pastries, and Cakes

Everything below is baked in small batches in our Brookfield kitchen. We do not bake to order on a large scale, so availability depends on the day. Items marked pre-order are made specifically for you and need at least one day's notice. There is no online checkout here — send us a request and we will confirm your order, total, and pickup time with you.

Breads

All of our breads are fermented overnight so that they have time to develop flavor. Prices are per loaf unless noted. Prices shown are typical ranges and can shift slightly with the cost of flour and seasonal fruit, so we will always confirm the final price before your pickup.

Signature Loaf

Our most requested bread and the one we are proudest of. Slow-fermented sourdough, built over two days, with a dark crackled crust and a soft open crumb inside. Deeply flavorful on its own and sturdy enough for a thick grilled cheese.

Price range: $7.00 – $8.50 per loaf. Pre-order recommended; we sell out most Saturdays.

Event and Catering Orders

We cater office breakfasts, school events, rehearsal dinners, and weddings within about fifteen miles of the bakery. Catering orders are pre-order only and need five days' notice. We deliver ourselves in the bakery van, and delivery is free on orders over $150 within Brookfield.

Tell us about your event and include your date, headcount, and any allergy notes, and we will send a quote.
