Fresh Bread, Made by Hand, Right Here on the Corner

Welcome to North Star Bakery

Every morning before sunrise, our bakers are already at work. Flour, water, salt, and a little patience go into every loaf, and we bake in small batches so that each one is handled with care. Nothing is rushed, nothing is skipped, and nothing leaves our oven unless we would happily serve it to our own families.

We have been baking for the families, commuters, and event planners of this neighborhood for over fifteen years. Whether you are grabbing a loaf on your way to work, picking up a birthday cake on Saturday, or ordering trays for a wedding, you will find the same thing at our counter: good bread made by people who know your name.

What We Are Known For

Our Signature Loaf is the bread we are most proud of and the one we are asked for most often. It is a slow-fermented sourdough with a deeply browned crust, an open crumb, and a gentle tang that makes it equally good on toast with butter and on its own with a bowl of soup. It sells out most Saturday mornings, so we recommend pre-ordering.

  • Slow-fermented sourdough, available in whole and half loaves
  • Butter croissants, morning buns, and seasonal fruit danishes
  • Celebration cakes, cupcakes, and pies for events and gatherings
  • Whole wheat, seeded, and gluten-reduced breads baked daily
  • Sandwich loaves and rolls for offices, picnics, and community events

Featured Item of the Week

Sunday Market Cinnamon Morning Buns

Each Saturday night we laminate our butter, roll it with brown sugar and toasted cinnamon, and cut it into buns that go into the oven before the market crowd arrives. They are caramelized on the outside, soft and pull-apart on the inside, and finished with a light glaze while still warm.

Price: $4.25 each, or $38 for a baker's dozen.

Cinnamon buns are the one item we do not take pre-orders for, because they sell out first. We bake two full trays every morning and one more on Saturdays. Arrive before 9 a.m. to be safe.

Hours and Location

We are on the corner of Alder Street and Northstar Lane, two doors down from the community library. There is a small lot behind the building, and the 14 bus stops directly in front of us, which makes us an easy stop for commuters and a popular lunch destination for people working nearby.
